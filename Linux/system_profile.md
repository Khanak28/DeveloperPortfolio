## Binary Number Representation

### 1. Decimal to Binary Conversion

Convert decimal 75 into binary.

| Division | Quotient | Remainder |
|---|---:|---:|
| 75 ÷ 2 | 37 | 1 |
| 37 ÷ 2 | 18 | 1 |
| 18 ÷ 2 | 9 | 0 |
| 9 ÷ 2 | 4 | 1 |
| 4 ÷ 2 | 2 | 0 |
| 2 ÷ 2 | 1 | 0 |
| 1 ÷ 2 | 0 | 1 |

Reading the remainders from bottom to top:

75 (Decimal) = 1001011 (Binary)

### 2. Binary to Decimal Conversion

Convert 110101 into decimal.

Using positional weights:

= (1 × 2^5) + (1 × 2^4) + (0 × 2^3) + (1 × 2^2) + (0 × 2^1) + (1 × 2^0)

= 32 + 16 + 0 + 4 + 0 + 1

= 53

Therefore, 110101 (Binary) = 53 (Decimal).

### 3. Why Computers Use Binary

Computers use binary internally because electronic circuits can reliably represent two states, ON (1) and OFF (0).
